Project approach

Design that's usable,
not just decorative.

The work emphasizes a clear hierarchy, mobile-friendly layout and accessible sections that communicate skills and experience quickly.

01 / STRUCTURE

Simple navigation

Clear routes to the introduction, selected projects and résumé content, supported by a compact menu on smaller devices.

02 / RESPONSIVENESS

Adaptable layouts

Flexible grids and spacing ensure that portfolio sections and project thumbnails can work across desktop and phone screens.

03 / PRESENTATION

Visual consistency

Consistent typography, yellow highlights and charcoal headings support quick scanning and a cohesive overall design.
